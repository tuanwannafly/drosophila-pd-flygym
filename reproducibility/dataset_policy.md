# Dataset Policy

Small, curated metadata and evidence reports may be version-controlled when
their paths and provenance are explicit. Large/raw rollout arrays, videos,
archives, and local caches are ignored by default.

Every curated dataset should provide:

- a stable identifier and version;
- source and license;
- configuration and source commit;
- SHA-256 or a manifest;
- validation status and missing-data notes;
- a statement of scientific scope.

No synthetic or placeholder data may be presented as experimental evidence.
