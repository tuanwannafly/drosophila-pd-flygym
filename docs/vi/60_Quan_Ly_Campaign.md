# 60. Quan ly campaign

Su dung `CampaignManager` de tao campaign tu YAML template, mo rong matrix,
ghi provenance, luu history va tao dashboard.

Trang thai gom `PLANNED`, `READY`, `WAITING_DATASET`, `QUEUED`, `RUNNING`,
`PAUSED`, `FAILED`, `COMPLETED`, `ARCHIVED`. Chuyen trang thai la thao tac
orchestration, khong phai lenh simulation.
