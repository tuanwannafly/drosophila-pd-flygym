# Xuat hinh bai bao

`PublicationAssetManager` chi dang ky figure va table da ton tai, sao chep
chung vao `publication/figures/` va `publication/tables/`, roi tao figure
manifest, table manifest va caption templates. Thu tu dang ky duoc danh so
tu Figure 1 va Table 1.

He thong khong tu ve figure tu du lieu thieu va khong thay doi ket qua khoa
hoc. Caption va manuscript section la metadata do caller cung cap.
