# Research Checklist

- [ ] Question and endpoint are defined before execution.
- [ ] Baseline/control and comparison design are explicit.
- [ ] Seed, duration, timestep, controller, environment, and configuration are
  fixed or declared.
- [ ] Computational observations are separated from biological interpretation.
- [ ] Unsupported endpoints and confounds are reported.
- [ ] No post-hoc tuning is used to obtain a desired result.
