# 136. Figure Specification

These are publication planning targets. They are not generated by Project A.
Every future figure must use a completed, manifest-backed experiment package,
preserve units and missingness, pass finite-value checks, and record source
commit and SHA-256.

| Figure | Source data | Existing producer | Caption template | Expected output | Formats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Declared thorax trajectory | `metrics/trajectory.py` | "Unperturbed computational baseline trajectory for the reported rollout(s)." | `trajectory_overview` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 2 | Declared pose/anatomy data | Existing anatomy/rollout outputs when present | "Digital Fly pose derived from the supplied computational rollout." | `digital_fly_pose` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 3 | Trajectory speed series | `metrics/trajectory.py`, `metrics/locomotion.py` | "Planar speed over time for the reported computational rollout(s)." | `velocity_profile` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 4 | Declared COM trajectory only | Existing rollout output if supplied | "COM movement, when the declared rollout contains a COM observable." | `com_movement` or `not_available` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 5 | Declared joint-angle actions | `metrics/locomotion.py` | "Joint-angle action distribution for the reported computational rollout(s)." | `joint_angle_distribution` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 6 | Bouts, pauses, turns, and optional open-field outputs | `metrics/bouts.py`, `metrics/turning.py`, `metrics/open_field.py`, assays | "Behavioral event summary from post-processed computational observables." | `behavior_timeline` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 7 | Validation and integrity reports | V7 adapter, V6-V9 reports, existing validation layers | "Software, data-integrity, and reproducibility checks for the package." | `validation_metrics` | PNG, SVG, PDF |
| 8 | Reviewed metrics, tables, and provenance | Existing report layers | "Summary of measured computational outputs and provenance." | `summary_panel` | PNG, SVG, PDF |

Figure 4 is conditional and must remain absent or explicitly unavailable when
COM data is not supplied. No figure may be used to claim Parkinson's disease
validation or biological equivalence.
