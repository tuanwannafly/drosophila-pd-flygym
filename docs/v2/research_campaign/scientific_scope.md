# Scientific Scope

The research campaign engine is computational infrastructure.

It can coordinate rollouts, analyze completed outputs, organize artifacts, and
generate reproducibility metadata. It does not create a Parkinson's disease
condition, does not model dopamine depletion, does not assign disease severity,
does not claim biological rescue, and does not validate mechanisms.

Campaign labels such as Healthy, Candidate, Progression, Intervention, and
Rescue are software-side condition labels. Their scientific interpretation
requires separately authorized evidence and conservative documentation.
