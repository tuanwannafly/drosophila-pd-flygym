# Session08 Mapping

Session08 is the computational progression and multi-condition comparison
session.

Expected Session08 work:

- configure Stage0-Stage4 computational progression timelines
- interpolate between stages and replay timelines reproducibly
- compare Healthy, Candidate, progression stages, and future intervention
  conditions
- compute trajectory, DTW, Fréchet, occupancy, gait, turning, and exploration
  similarity
- export synchronized split-screen or quad-view playback with trajectory,
  speed, heading, gait, contact, metadata, and timestamp overlays

Session08 stages are computational labels only and must not be described as
biological disease stages.
