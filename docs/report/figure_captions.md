# Figure Captions

## Figure 1: E1 parameter response

**`e1_parameter_response.png`.** Simulated planar displacement, mean planar
speed, and related response quantities across the motor-vigor proxy and
coordination proxy scales. Motor scaling produces a graded locomotor-output
reduction and exact action-magnitude scaling in the tested range. Coupling
reductions are modest at intermediate values and show a large displacement
loss and yaw deviation at zero coupling. These are computational response
surfaces, not biological severity curves.

## Figure 2: E2 condition comparison

**`e2_condition_comparison.png`.** Comparison of the control, motor-only,
coordination-only, and combined conditions in the frozen E2 matrix. Speed and
displacement effects are mostly close to additive, whereas directional effects
are more nonlinear. The figure does not identify a disease condition.

## Figure 3: E3 paired-seed robustness

**`e3_paired_seed_robustness.png`.** Baseline and frozen-candidate speed and
path-length outputs for paired seeds 0 through 4. The same seed is used within
each pair, and each condition uses fresh simulation state. Candidate
displacement and speed deltas are negative for all five seeds. `ROBUST` here
means computational/software robustness under the tested seeds only.

## Figure 4: E5 computational reversibility

**`e5_computational_reversibility.png`.** Mean planar speed and planar path
length for the frozen candidate, motor partial restoration, coordination
partial restoration, combined partial restoration, and full computational
restoration reference. Motor and combined restorations move the primary
endpoints directionally toward control in the tested conditions; coordination
restoration is mixed. This is computational reversibility, not biological
rescue or treatment response.
